About this library

One collector's catalogue, kept public so that other collectors can check a detail rather than guess at it.

What is recorded

Each entry lists the issuing office, the date the item was released or the cancellation introduced, and a short description of what it depicts. Where a variety exists — a second die, a different ink, a re-issue — it is noted on the same entry rather than split into a new one.

Where the material comes from

Most items are from the collection held here and were scanned directly. A smaller number were photographed by other collectors and shared for inclusion; those entries carry the contributor's name at the foot of the description.

How to use it

Every section has a search box that matches on office, state and motif at once, so a partial spelling is usually enough. Filters narrow by region. Nothing here is priced or valued — this is a record of what exists, not what it fetches.

Corrections

Dates get transcribed wrongly and descriptions get out of date. If something here is wrong, send the correction with a scan if you have one and it will be amended with credit.

Get in touch
